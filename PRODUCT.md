# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary reader owns a consequential AI-enabled workflow and is deciding whether a bounded reliability diagnostic is worth a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Galactos exists because an AI workflow can report completion while the underlying business outcome is still unresolved. The landing page makes that gap visible and offers a bounded diagnostic as the next step. Success is a reader who can tell whether that diagnostic applies to their workflow.

## Positioning

Galactos follows consequential AI-enabled workflows from trigger to verified business completion. A neighboring tool that only trusts a model's own "done" signal could not make that claim.

## Operating Context

The public surface is one long-form landing page. The reader moves from the false "done" through the failure, the mechanism, a different standard for done, whether it applies beyond one domain, whether it is their situation, what the engagement is, what they leave with, how it is done, why to trust it, and what to do next.
